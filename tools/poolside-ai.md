## What Is Collected

**Per privacy policy (last updated 2026-07-21):**
- Contact data, device/IP data, web analytics, IP-derived geolocation
- Information you provide, including personal information included in inputs to the Services
- Listed purpose includes "training our machine learning models (unless you opt-out)"

**Enterprise deployments:** governed by a Data Processing Addendum; on-prem / VPC deployments keep data on customer infrastructure.

**Product telemetry:** not documented.

## What Is NOT Collected

- On-prem deployments keep data on customer infrastructure

## How to Fully Opt Out

1. **On-prem / VPC deployment** for enterprise customers
2. **Training opt-out:** referenced in the privacy policy but the mechanism is not described; contact privacy@poolside.ai

## Red Flags

- Privacy policy lists 'training our machine learning models (unless you opt-out)' without describing the opt-out mechanism
- No public telemetry documentation for the product
- Closed source platform

**Notes:** Primarily a foundation-model company with on-prem/VPC enterprise deployments. Telemetry default remains undocumented.
